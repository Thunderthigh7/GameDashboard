Easy analytics. Powerful integrations.

Your analytics shouldn’t stop at the dashboard.

RoAnalytics makes your game data easy to explore through clear reports, flexible funnels, and map-based insights. Because you own the data, you can connect it to custom integrations that schedule actions or respond automatically to live conditions across your game.

Official Roblox OAuth

Roblox handles sign-in. RoAnalytics never asks for your Roblox password or private account details.

We only confirm the account and that it owns the game.
Why RoAnalytics?

Understand your data. Own it. Put it to work.

01

Easy to understand

Explore events, funnels, player movement, and map activity through one clear dashboard designed around how your game works.

02

Yours to control

Keep ownership of the analytics data your game sends. Store it, query it, export it, and use it beyond the RoAnalytics dashboard.

03

Built for action

Connect your data to custom integrations that schedule events, react to live conditions, and coordinate actions across active game servers.

Use RoAnalytics alongside Roblox Analytics for acquisition, retention, monetization, and other platform-level metrics.

Set up RoAnalytics in four steps.

    Advanced profile systems

    Optional custom adapter

    The plugin's automatic mode supports standard keys containing numbers, strings, booleans, arrays, or objects. Games using session locks, multiple keys, or transformed data should override it with their trusted server API.

    local RoAnalytics = require(game.ServerScriptService.RoAnalytics.API)

RoAnalytics.RegisterPlayerDataAdapter({
    Read = function(userId, context)
        return DataService.GetOfflineData(userId)
    end,
    Write = function(userId, newData, context)
        return DataService.SetOfflineData(userId, newData, context.expectedVersion)
    end,
})

    A custom adapter replaces automatic direct access and may safely support online players when your framework owns the active profile.
